# My Life Between France and Germany

> *"Bridges are built not just between places, but between ideas, cultures, and people."*

---

## Early Life and Roots

I was born on **April 23, 1986**, in the quiet, leafy suburb of **Fontenay-aux-Roses**, just southwest of Paris. The son of a literature teacher and a civil engineer, I grew up in a household where intellectual curiosity and practical problem-solving were equally valued. My childhood was marked by long summer trips to the Atlantic coast of Brittany, where I developed a lifelong love for the sea, and by the bustling energy of Paris, which shaped my urban sensibilities.

From an early age, I displayed a keen interest in **technology and storytelling**. I spent hours dismantling old radios and computers, only to painstakingly reassemble them—sometimes with mixed success. At the same time, I devoured books, from classic French literature to science fiction, fueling a vivid imagination that would later define my creative pursuits.

## Education: A Foundation for the Future

I attended **Lycée Lakanal** in Sceaux, a prestigious high school known for its rigorous academic environment. There, I excelled in both the sciences and humanities, but it was my passion for **mathematics and computer science** that ultimately guided my path. After graduating with honors, I enrolled at **École Polytechnique (X)**, one of France’s most elite *grandes écoles*, where I earned a degree in **Applied Mathematics and Computer Science** in 2008.

During my time at Polytechnique, I became fascinated by the emerging field of **machine learning**. I spent a semester abroad at the **Technical University of Berlin** as part of an exchange program, where I first encountered the city that would later become my home. My thesis, *"Neural Networks for Predictive Text Generation,"* foreshadowed my future work at the intersection of AI and human creativity.

## Career: From Paris to Berlin

### Early Beginnings in France

After graduating, I joined **Capgemini** in Paris as a data scientist, where I worked on cutting-edge projects in **natural language processing (NLP)**. My work on sentiment analysis for social media platforms earned me recognition within the company, and I quickly rose to lead a team of young engineers.

However, my restlessness and desire for new challenges led me to explore opportunities beyond France. In 2012, I took a leap of faith and moved to **San Francisco** to join a fledgling AI startup, **DeepMind Labs** (no relation to Google’s DeepMind). There, I contributed to early experiments in **reinforcement learning**, working alongside some of the brightest minds in the field.

### The Berlin Chapter

In **September 2016**, I made a life-changing decision: I moved to **Berlin**, drawn by its vibrant tech scene, affordable cost of living, and the city’s reputation as a hub for innovation and creativity. I settled in **Neukölln**, where I initially worked as a freelance AI consultant, advising startups on how to integrate machine learning into their products.

By 2018, I co-founded **Lumen AI**, a Berlin-based company specializing in **AI-driven creative tools**. The company’s flagship product, *Muse*, was an AI-powered platform that helped writers, designers, and musicians overcome creative blocks by generating ideas, refining drafts, and even composing music. As CTO, I led the company from a small team of five to over 50 employees, with clients ranging from independent artists to major media corporations.

In 2023, I transitioned to a role as **Head of AI Innovation at SoundCloud**, where I now lead a team developing **generative AI tools for musicians**, helping them create, remix, and distribute their work in revolutionary ways.

## Personal Life: Love, Family, and Culture

My personal life is as rich and dynamic as my professional one. In 2015, while still in San Francisco, I reconnected with **Anne-Sophie Plaisant**, a childhood friend from Fontenay-aux-Roses. Our relationship blossomed quickly, and we decided to move to Berlin together. On **July 29, 2017**, we were married in an intimate ceremony at the **Town Hall of Fontenay-aux-Roses**, surrounded by family and close friends.

We welcomed our first son, **Aimé Tomi Federico Boris Plaisant Hocquet**, on **October 22, 2021**, in Berlin. Aimé, whose name reflects our multicultural roots (Tomi is a Finnish nickname, Federico is Italian, and Boris is Slavic), is already showing signs of inheriting my love for technology and Anne-Sophie’s artistic flair. Our second son, **Raoul Colin Désiré Melchior Plaisant Hocquet**, was born on **December 1, 2023**, completing our family. Raoul holds dual **French and German citizenship**, a symbol of our deep ties to both countries.

Our family resides at **Hussitenstraße 68, 13355 Berlin**, in the diverse and lively neighborhood of **Wedding**. Our home is a blend of French elegance and Berlin’s eclectic charm, filled with books, musical instruments, and the laughter of our two young boys.

## Passions and Interests

### Music

I am an avid **pianist and composer**. I began playing the piano at the age of 7 and have since composed several original pieces, blending classical influences with modern electronic sounds. I am particularly fascinated by the intersection of **AI and music**, and I often experiment with using machine learning to generate new melodies or harmonies.

### Literature and Writing

Inspired by my love for storytelling, I have written several short stories and am currently working on a **science fiction novel** set in a near-future Berlin where AI and human creativity collide. I am a regular contributor to *The Offing*, an online literary magazine, where I publish essays on the ethical implications of AI.

### Travel

Anne-Sophie and I love to travel, often spending summers in **Brittany** or exploring new cities in Europe. Our most memorable trip was a three-month sabbatical in **Japan** in 2019, where I worked remotely while our family immersed ourselves in Japanese culture. I am fluent in **French, English, and German**, and I am currently learning **Japanese**.

### Cooking

A self-proclaimed *foodie*, I enjoy cooking elaborate meals for my family and friends. My specialty is **French-German fusion cuisine**, and I am known for my *Bœuf Bourguignon* with a Berlin twist—using locally sourced ingredients from the **Markthalle Neun** market.

## Philosophy and Values

I am deeply committed to **ethical AI development**. I believe that technology should serve humanity, not the other way around, and I am a vocal advocate for **transparency, fairness, and accountability** in machine learning. I have given talks at **TEDxBerlin** and **re:publica** on the future of AI and its societal impact.

I am also passionate about **education** and mentor young engineers and data scientists through programs like **AI for Good** and **Girls in Tech Berlin**. In my free time, I volunteer as a **coding instructor** at a local community center, teaching refugees and underprivileged youth the basics of programming.

## Legacy and Future

At 38, I am at the height of my career and personal life. I dream of one day founding a **non-profit research lab** dedicated to using AI for social good, particularly in the fields of **education and healthcare**. I also hope to publish my novel and, perhaps, compose a full-length album.

But above all, my greatest pride is my family. I often say that my sons, Aimé and Raoul, are my most important "projects"—ones that I hope will grow up to be **curious, kind, and brave**, just like their parents.

As I look to the future, I remain guided by the same principles that have defined my life: **curiosity, creativity, and a deep belief in the power of human connection**.
